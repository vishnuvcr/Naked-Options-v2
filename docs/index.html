Data and method

  • 4,901 NIFTY daily rows, 2007-01-02 through 2026-10-13.
  • Seven validated auxiliary series: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Nikkei 225, Hang Seng, CBOE VIX, India VIX proxy and US 10-year yield proxy.
  • Chronological 80/20 split with two expanding validation folds.
  • Corrected forward-volatility labels; no null target dates in final OOS predictions.
  • Independent tester reproduced skill metrics, confidence intervals, Holm corrections and ARIMA benchmark calculations.

Limitations

Historical option chains, PCR, FII/DII, breadth, timestamped news, point-in-time constituents and corporate-action features were not verified as acquired. No options strategy, option-chain backtest, brokerage/spread/tax/slippage simulation, Paytm Money execution analysis or live deployment was performed. Results cover one holdout period only.
