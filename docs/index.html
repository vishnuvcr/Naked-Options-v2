Data and method

  • 4,906 Dhan daily NIFTY 50 rows from 2007-01-02 to 2026-10-21; target validation passed.
  • Seven auxiliary series passed validation: S&P 500, Nasdaq, Nikkei 225, Hang Seng, CBOE VIX, India VIX and a US 10-year yield proxy.
  • Chronological 80/20 split; four expanding validation folds for tabular models and two for sequence models, with horizon purging.
  • 647 merged metric rows; 299 OOS candidate comparisons; 296 CV-pass rows; 52 compute-budget-blocked rows.
  • Familywise Holm audit across all 299 OOS comparisons: 85 significant differences, 77 significantly worse than baseline and eight improvements, all for one-session volatility-proxy QLIKE.

Limitations

NSE official retrieval returned no recognized data rows; USD/INR and gold failed OHLC validation; WTI had non-positive OHLC and was excluded. Twenty weekend-dated target rows remain flagged for exchange-calendar review. Options, FII/DII, PCR, breadth, timestamped news and point-in-time corporate-action data were not verified as acquired. This is a single holdout and no independent-period replication has been run. No option-chain backtest, brokerage/spread/tax/slippage simulation, Paytm Money execution analysis or live deployment was performed.
